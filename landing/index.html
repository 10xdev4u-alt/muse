Three moves.
No internet.

One 731 MB model arrives over Wi-Fi the first time you open Mind. After that, the radio is dead weight — Muse never calls home.

01 / POUR

Pour it out

Type raw. Your words stay yours — plain text, saved locally, rendered exactly as written.

02 / REFLECT

Get reflected

The model mirrors what you wrote. It doesn't lecture, diagnose, or cheerlead — it hands your own thought back to you, sharper.

03 / GROUND

See your day honestly

Once daily it reads your actual task history and leaves three grounded prompts about how today really went.

Privacy is structural.

Not a policy page. Architecture.

what leaves the device

One model download, once, started by you over Wi-Fi. SHA256-pinned against a hash committed in the repo, verified before load. After that: zero network calls, forever. This page makes more requests than the app does in its whole life.

your thoughts

Local database, Android encrypted app storage. Ride the app's backup/export.

inference location

On-device CPU via llama.cpp. Unloadable from Settings in one tap.

who can read it

Whoever passes the biometric or PIN lock. Same gate as everything else.

analytics

None. Not on this page either — the only scripts here render the stars.
